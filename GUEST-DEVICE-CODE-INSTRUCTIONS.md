### 📱 On a Phone or Tablet

Phones don’t easily show the console, so on mobile please use the **⭐ Easy Way** link above — it’s made exactly for phones. The backup console method below is mainly for computers.

### 💻 On a Computer (Windows PC or Mac)

First, open **https://kumenya-mucii-rsvp.netlify.app** in your browser, then:

#### Google Chrome (PC or Mac)
1. Press **F12** on your keyboard.
   *(On a Mac you can also press **Option (⌥) + Command (⌘) + J**.)*
2. Click the **“Console”** tab at the top of the panel that opens.
3. Click in the empty line next to the `>` sign, **paste** the line of code above, and press **Enter**.
4. A pop-up shows your **number** — send it to us.

#### Mozilla Firefox (PC or Mac)
1. Press **F12** on your keyboard.
   *(On a Mac you can also press **Option (⌥) + Command (⌘) + K**.)*
2. Click the **“Console”** tab.
3. Click the empty line, **paste** the code above, press **Enter**.
4. A pop-up shows your **number** — send it to us.

#### Safari (Mac only)
Safari hides this feature by default, so there’s a one-time setup:
1. In the top menu click **Safari → Settings** (older Macs: **Preferences**).
2. Go to the **“Advanced”** tab and tick **“Show features for web developers”** (older Macs: **“Show Develop menu in menu bar”**). Close settings.
3. Now in the top menu click **Develop → Show JavaScript Console**.
4. Click the empty line, **paste** the code above, press **Enter**.
5. A pop-up (or a line in the console) shows your **number** — send it to us.
